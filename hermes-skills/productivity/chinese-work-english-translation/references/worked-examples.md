# 已交付翻译样例（用户原话 → 书面英语）

保留用户真实原话，便于下次同场景直接复用句型和固定译法。人名/项目代号按用户拼写为准。

---

## 1. 沟通结果 + 时间预期

**原话**：「昨天跟曹正龙沟通了王兆阳撤离812现场的时间，暂时还是要到十月底或者十一月底，我们可以按照11月底的预期」

**Written English**
> Yesterday I spoke with Cao Zhenglong about the timing of Wang Zhaoyang's withdrawal from the 812 site. For the time being, it remains late October or late November; we can plan on late November.

- `or`：`... about when Wang Zhaoyang will be demobilized from the 812 site.`（demobilize = 人员/设备撤场，工程语境比 withdraw 更专）
- `or`：`we can proceed on the assumption that it will be late November.`（更明确「按此假设推进」，适合写进计划）

**关键词**
| 英文 | 中文 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| withdraw from / withdrawal from | 从…撤出 | *The team will withdraw from the site next month.* |
| demobilize / demobilization | 撤场、撤离（工程语境） | *The demobilization is scheduled for late November.* |
| for the time being, it remains … | 暂时还是… | *For the time being, it remains late November.* |
| plan on / proceed on the assumption that | 按…预期推进 | *We can plan on late November.* |

**待确认**：「812」按现场/项目编号 → *the 812 site*；若是日期或客户代号需纠正。

## 2. 对两个角色的评价

**原话**：「我认为孙经理和向燕飞就是一个在当好人一个在当坏人」

**Written English**
> I think Manager Sun and Xiang Yanfei are playing good cop and bad cop.

- `or`：`... are playing complementary roles — one conciliatory, one firm.`（书面/对外安全版）
- `or`：`... are taking the roles of the good cop and the bad cop respectively.`

**关键词**
| 英文 | 中文 | 例句 |
|---|---|---|
| play good cop, bad cop | 一个唱红脸一个唱白脸 | *They're playing good cop, bad cop on the price.* |
| play the good guy / the bad guy | 当好人／当坏人（更口语） | *I don't want to play the bad guy here.* |
| I think / My reading is that / It appears to me that | 我认为（语气由强到弱） | *My reading is that this is a deliberate split.* |

**风险提醒**：`good cop, bad cop` 在英语里**默认含义是「合演一出戏」**，等于说双方在演给他看。口头吐槽可以；**写进邮件/汇报会变成对同事的指控** → 建议书面用 complementary roles 版。

---

## 译法约定（累积）

- 人名：拼音连写、不改序 —— 曹正龙 `Cao Zhenglong`；向燕飞 `Xiang Yanfei`；职务补在前面 —— 孙经理 `Manager Sun`
- 现场/项目编号：`the 812 site`（若确认是日期/客户代号再改）
- 称呼领导：中文「X 总」→ 英语按对方习惯 `Mr. X` / `Manager X`，不硬译「总」
